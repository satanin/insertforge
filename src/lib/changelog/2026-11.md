### v1.2.41 — KALLAX planning and consistent interface

- Added a KALLAX opening planner with a real 10-single / 1-double starter layout, selectable drawers in 2D and 3D, and optional front/rear alignment of the complete assembly.
- Added project-wide drawer length and identification-window options, explicit per-drawer custom settings, splitting a double into two singles and merging compatible adjacent singles while preserving cell positions.
- Prevented adding drawers when they cannot fit the opening; modules outside it have readable warnings and direct deletion controls. The KALLAX fit preset preserves the chosen fitting margin.
- Unified New Beta's light/dark themes and added independent Balanced/Compact density preferences. Fields share sizing and backgrounds; action pairs use equal widths, export dimensions stay readable, and action help appears on the button itself.
- Kept Classic, project geometry and the existing viewer layout. Moved the KALLAX reference to the inspector and reserved space for the orientation cube so camera-view buttons remain accessible in Selected module.
- Front-open shell dovetail sockets remain a future study; the printed connector geometry is unchanged.

### v1.2.40 — Adjustable card access

- Added adjustable finger cutout depth to standalone Card Divider trays and open Card Storage trays, including an option to close the openings.
- New Card Storage trays use 1.5 mm walls and floors; saved dimensions are preserved.
- Fixed selected Card Storage body colours when switching between geometries with and without vertex colours.

### v1.2.39 — Modular shelf storage

- Added Shelf storage projects with compatible single and double-width card drawers, shells, removable handles and open frames on a shared cell grid. Includes the Kallax 4×3 Vertical reference project and protected system dimensions.
- Added optional identification windows, closed fronts, two label windows and wide handles for double drawers. Double drawers can include a longitudinal divider or an open interior.
- Added print-volume splitting with Dovetail drawer joints or Lap and keys. Double drawers use two Dovetails; female divider walls are 7 mm. Shells include the required joining keys and additional mating clearance.
- Added assembled dimensions, card previews that respect joint dividers, per-part sets, module plate previews and complete project multi-plate 3MF export for Bambu Studio.
- Single drawers, loaded sleeved cards and shell connections have been physically tested; double shells fit previously printed modules. The latest double-drawer wall reduction, divider extension and handle/window variants still need physical validation. These trials do not establish a rated load.
