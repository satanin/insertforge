### v1.2.37

- Added rotating hover previews for Dice Tray and Card Storage in the Add loose tray menu.

### v1.2.36

- Open Card Storage trays can keep cards upright at 90°. The height limit applies to the tray, and the preview shows how far the cards extend above it. Adding a sliding lid switches back to tilting the cards to fit.

### v1.2.35

- Card Storage now has rounded front and rear openings for gripping cards. The openings are enabled by default and can be turned off in Manufacturing.
- The rear support for tilted cards can now be turned off in Manufacturing. It remains enabled by default.
