### v1.2.11

- Reverted the Card Storage text experiments introduced in v1.2.6 through v1.2.10 after slicer testing showed they did not solve missing curved glyphs.
- Divider labels return to the original rounded line geometry, 1.2 mm stroke and 0.4 mm two-layer inlay.
- Card Storage lid text again uses the shared Box text implementation, and 3MF export returns to one text body per divider or lid.

### v1.2.10

- Divider plates and their text holes are now constructed as one 2D profile and extruded once, removing the open edges produced by 3D text subtraction.
- Divider text is a full-thickness multicolour insert that shares its exact boundary with the plate, preserving the inner islands of closed letters without gaps.
- Bambu Studio now reports both plate and text as manifold for MEGAN, KYORYU and GABRIEL.

### v1.2.9

- Restored the original rounded line lettering for Card Storage after the 5×7 font failed to improve slicing and looked too mechanical.
- Card Storage text now uses manifold capsule strokes with a 2 mm printable width instead of expanded line contours around 1.2 mm wide.
- Divider inlays keep their 0.6 mm depth and gain 0.3 mm of cavity clearance; Card Storage lid lettering uses the same wider stroke while regular Box lettering remains unchanged.

### v1.2.8

- Tested a solid 5×7 font for Card Storage divider labels and lid lettering while preserving accents.
- Slicer testing showed no improvement and the block appearance was rejected; v1.2.9 supersedes this experiment.

### v1.2.7

- Curved text glyphs such as O, G and C are now built from simple overlapping rounded strokes instead of one complex expanded contour.
- The robust stroke geometry is used by removable-divider labels and the main sliding-lid text, in both engraved and multicolour modes.
- Divider inlays retain the 0.6 mm depth from the previous print trial while the glyph-generation defect is tested independently.

### v1.2.6

- Removable-divider text inlays are now 0.6 mm deep, giving three colour layers with a typical 0.2 mm layer height instead of two.
- The visible letter size and stroke remain unchanged for a focused print test.

### v1.2.5

- Card Storage sliding lids now require enough wall material to keep the guide connected and printable.
- Existing lidded Card Storage projects with 2 mm walls are upgraded to the 3 mm Box wall minimum when loaded.
- The required wall thickness also grows with lid clearance so at least 0.6 mm of continuous material remains behind the groove.

### v1.2.4

- Each removable Card Storage divider now has its own visible colour swatch and hexadecimal colour field.
- The default divider colour remains available for existing dividers and is copied into newly added dividers.
- Individual divider colours are preserved in preview and 3MF exports.

### v1.2.3

- Card Storage lids now always slide along the card-storage direction, including boxes whose exterior length is shorter than their card width.
- Removable dividers are rotated around their upright axis so labels face the opening without mirrored text or reversed left/right tabs.
- Physical closure fit remains pending print validation.

### v1.2.2

- Card Storage closure selector: open tray or sliding lid using the existing Box shell, rails and ramp lock.
- Optional lid lettering with separate multicolour inlay, preserved when switching closure modes.
- New Card Storage defaults use 3 mm walls, floor and lid; saved thicknesses are preserved.
- Total height accounts for the actual lid plate thickness and clearance. Independent part exports include lid text.
- Empty Box shells no longer add tray-retaining walls in custom-size gaps that interfere with the lid.
- Physical load capacity and closure fit await print validation.

### v1.2.1

- Removed the bridge above the Card Storage lid entry, keeping the lateral guides.
- Card Storage dividers now have an independent, configurable colour in preview and 3MF.

### v1.2.0

#### Card Storage

- Added a loose tray with one continuous card cavity and separately printable removable dividers.
- Size by total card count or fixed exterior length, with estimated capacity including divider thickness.
- Automatic tilt and an end support accommodate a maximum total height, including tabs and the optional sliding lid.
- Blank or labelled dividers with left, centre, right or full-width tabs; multicolour text uses separate inlay bodies in 3MF assemblies.
- Export individual parts, all dividers or the complete set as STL/ZIP or 3MF. Dividers are laid flat for printing.
- Physical fit, lid clearance and print results await user validation.
