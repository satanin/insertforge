### v1.2.23

- Added a central game-component inventory with direct row editing, duplication, optional measurements and selectors for cards, tokens/tiles, dice, boards and miniatures.
- New card groups inherit the first group's format and sleeve choice, with editable thickness and full-stack measurements.
- Assign groups and partial quantities from each piece editor; see where components are already assigned. Fully allocated groups appear checked and disabled.
- Apply inventory dimensions explicitly, with stale-measurement warnings and manual configuration preserved. Assignments persist through project saves and JSON exports without duplicating stock when pieces are copied.
- Choose Top-loaded or Edge-loaded tokens per group, including lengthwise/crosswise edge orientation. Dice can be assigned to Cup Trays using conservative rectangular envelopes.

### v1.2.22

- Improved Card Divider generation by reducing excessive text tessellation and adapting finger-cutout resolution to a maximum 0.02 mm curve error.
- Reuse the last generated Card Divider geometry when parameters, card sizes and text options are unchanged; the cache retains only one tray.
- A four-deck labelled reference case generated about 4.7 times faster with roughly 73% fewer polygons in local measurements. Text stroke width, depth and orientation are preserved; physical print validation remains pending.

### v1.2.21

- Added lid visibility and explode controls to Miniature Tray preview, plus dimensions in individual tray and full-project views.
- Improved compact model packing with individual 90° rotations, paired width/depth searches and balanced rows; corrected rotated dimensions from the packing library.
- New projects default to 250 × 250 mm. New miniature sockets default to 26 mm bases, 8 mm posts, 1.5 mm walls/floor and slightly looser base clearance with thinner retaining tabs. Existing saved dimensions are preserved.
- Socket spacing supports larger miniature overhangs. Label plates fit the text with bounded side extensions; the default label is Mini, with printable-size validation retained.
- Reduced repeated geometry work with bounded caches, worker-based lid previews and local engraving/drilling operations. Further performance review starts with Card Dividers.
- Miniature Tray remains Experimental; new fits and label margins still need physical validation.

### v1.2.20

- Miniature tray lids now share text controls with socket labels: engraved, flush multicolour inlay, raised lettering and text colour.
- Lid text remains a separate grouped body in 3MF exports, including lid-only exports. Preview preserves text colours; STL combines the parts without colour.
- Total height includes raised lid text, and its print orientation keeps lettering facing upwards. Raised text affects the flat stacking surface; physical print validation remains pending.
- Standardised existing text-mode names in Box and Layered Box without changing their sliding-lid geometry.

### v1.2.19

- Added optional socket label plates bounded by socket diameter and existing spacing, with per-model names or abbreviations and conservative nozzle/letter-size validation. Text never shrinks automatically to fit.
- Choose engraved, flush multicolour inlay or raised label text, with a colour selector and separate grouped text body in 3MF exports.
- Approximate miniature overhang over its own label now produces a clearance warning rather than incorrectly blocking otherwise fitting text.
- Improved mixed-model packing by searching smaller footprints when dimensions are not fixed; the six-model reference case uses about 22% less area. Fixed dimensions and fill mode are preserved.
- Miniature trays remain Experimental. Physical testing of labels, text modes and the new layouts is pending.

### v1.2.18

- Added an optional flat lid for Miniature trays, located on the stacking posts, with configurable thickness and total height validation. Export the rack and lid together or individually.
- Added optional engraved lid text following the tray name or a custom label, with corrected reading orientation and a flat stacking surface.
- Added individual STL/3MF export controls for trays, boxes, layered boxes, internal layers and sections. Boards can export their solid reference volume; Card Storage retains its dedicated exporter.
- New Miniature trays lighten the floor below each base by default, preserving saved solid-floor settings.
- Initial rack print tests confirmed by the user. Lid fit and load capacity still require physical validation; Miniature tray remains Experimental.
